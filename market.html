Market

Buying and selling goods, supply-and-demand stock pricing, the transfer fee, and the refresh cycle.

Station Modal Tabs

Opening a home base or owned ship yard shows a secondary tab row: Storage | Market | Shop | Upgrades.

  • Storage — view and sell your inventory, and trigger refinery and production runs if upgrades are installed.
  • Market — buy and sell goods, and view the current price and stock of every raw material, refined material, and commodity at this station. Prices move with supply and demand and re-roll on the 6-hour refresh cycle.
  • Shop — buy a curated shortlist of consumer goods (Fresh Produce, Food Rations, Medical Supplies, Recycled Materials, Waste Containers) directly into local storage. Like any market buy, it charges the flat transfer fee; the Market tab can buy anything the station stocks.
  • Upgrades — purchase and manage station upgrades (Refinery, Production Array, Drone Bay, and others).

Selling Materials

Mined materials are stored at your home base (or the nearest owned ship yard) automatically when a mining run completes. Open a station and go to the Market tab to sell materials at the current market rate.

Market Prices

Each station sets a quoted base price for every good, randomised within a 70%–130% range of that good's base value. A ƒ5,000/CU rare material is quoted anywhere between ƒ3,500 and ƒ6,500 depending on the station.

The price you actually pay or receive then moves with the station's stock of that good (see Supply, Demand & Stock below): a well-stocked good trades near its quote, a depleted one costs more, a flooded one pays less.

Quotes and stock both refresh every 6 hours. The countdown is shown on the Market tab, and each good's row lists its live price alongside how many SU are in stock. If the timer reaches zero while the screen is open, the market updates automatically.

Four goods are missing from every lawful station's market. Narcotics, Munitions, Biological Goods and Hacked Tech are sold only at The Market, the Eclipse Syndicate's station, and hauling them into lawful space means running the interdiction check at the far dock. See Contraband & Smuggling.

Buying from the Market

The Market tab lets you buy as well as sell. Buying is available at any station where you hold storage (your home base or a ship yard you own), and purchases land straight in that station's storage. Every buy carries a flat ƒ1,000 transfer fee, shown on its own line before you confirm.

You can only buy what a station has in stock. Buying draws that stock down and pushes the price up as demand rises, and the buy price always sits a little above the sell price, so selling into a market and immediately buying back is always a loss.

Contraband is stocked only at The Market. Narcotics, Munitions, Biological Goods and Hacked Tech can be bought only at The Market, the Eclipse Syndicate's station. Selling them at a lawful station never adds them to that station's buy stock.

Supply, Demand & Stock

Every good at every station carries a finite stock. Rarer, pricier goods are stocked in smaller amounts: common ore runs to a few hundred SU, while a rare material may hold only a couple of dozen. Stock is the single supply-and-demand number that drives the price:

  • Selling floods the market. Each unit you sell raises the stock and lowers the price, so dumping a huge haul at one station pays less and less per unit.
  • Buying drains the market. Each unit you buy lowers the stock and raises the price, so buying out a scarce good gets steadily more expensive.
  • Scarcity swings harder. Because a rare good holds far less stock, its price reacts much more sharply to a single trade than a common one does.
Don't flood one station. Spread large hauls across several markets, or wait for the 6-hour refresh, which re-rolls every station's stock and restores prices toward their quotes.

Ship Yards & Transfer Runs

Once you own a ship yard at a second station (see Ship Yards), you can transfer materials between stations using freight ships. This lets you move inventory to a station with higher prices or less supply depression.

Transfer runs also carry risk. The risk is based on the total value of the transferred cargo — higher value loads attract more pirate attention.
