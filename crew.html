Crew & Pilots

Every ship needs a pilot to fly. Who you hire changes how it flies.

The Basics

A ship is just a hull until it has a pilot. A ship with no pilot cannot be sent on anything — no contracts, no mining, no salvage, no raids, and it cannot even be relocated to another base. Hire crew at a station's Personnel desk.

One pilot per ship, for now. Each ship carries a single crew slot. Higher-tier ships are planned to add more seats over time.

Hiring at Personnel

Open the Map, tap a station where you have a presence (your home base or any shipyard you own), and choose Personnel. It has three tabs:

  • Flight Crew — pilots looking for work at this station. Each shows their faction, their traits, and a signing bonus.
  • On Contract — everyone you employ. Assign a pilot to a docked ship, bench them, or dismiss them.
  • Researchers — hire researchers for the R&D Lab (see Research).

Each eligible station keeps its own list of 7 pilots and 3 researchers, and the lists turn over on a timer — dock somewhere new to see different people.

Cost: Signing Bonus + Wages

Hiring costs a one-time signing bonus paid from credits on hand. The bonus is set by the pilot's traits:

  • Base ƒ5,000
  • +ƒ1,000 for each positive trait
  • −ƒ1,000 for each negative trait
  • +ƒ5,000 for a super-rare trait

So a cheap, flawed pilot can cost as little as ƒ2,000 — a fine way to get off the ground. Pilots also draw wages, which fold into a ship's upkeep only while it is on a contract. An idle fleet costs nothing, and a more expensive pilot costs more to run.

Debt covers wages. Like all maintenance, wages can be carried by a lien if you run short — it is a loan against future runs, not a hard stop.

Where Crew Live

A pilot lives at the station where you hired them. They can only board a ship docked at that station. While assigned, they travel with the ship; if you swap them out for someone else, they catch a ride back home. Selling a ship benches its pilot (you keep them), but if a ship is destroyed, its pilot is lost for good — insurance replaces the hull, never the crew.

Faction & Loyalty

You choose your own faction at the start of the game. Pilots come from the four lawful factions (never the Eclipse Syndicate). A pilot's faction is mostly flavour, except where a trait keys off it — a Loyalist only flies contracts for their own faction, and a pilot with a Grudge refuses one particular faction's work.

Traits

Every pilot rolls 1–3 traits (at most one super-rare). Traits are shown before you hire, so you always know what you're buying. Effects are deliberately modest — a trait tilts a ship's performance, it doesn't transform it. Match the pilot to the job: a Rock Nerd is wasted on a freighter, and a Loyalist is dead weight if you fly other factions' contracts.
